8So what

Three calls for the client brief

The gaps above aren't trivia: they point to different media, different offers, and a different read on "success" depending on which audience a sponsor is actually trying to reach.

Budget · 01

Lead with the offer, not the story

60% vs 44%

Trade responds far harder to discounts and prize draws than to purpose-led messaging ("supports grassroots football" barely moves the needle). For trade-facing creative, put the tangible offer in the headline, not buried under brand narrative.

Channel · 02

Sponsor the room, not the feed

48% vs 25%

Trade engagement is physical: pubs, workplaces, watch-parties. Social follow and content engagement run in the opposite direction. Shift trade media spend toward trade-counter activations, workplace sweepstakes, and on-site screenings over paid social.

Interpretation · 03

Don't mistake calm for cold

66% vs 47%

Trade reports lower excitement on every sentiment question, yet shows up for more of the tournament than the public. If a brand tracker leads with "excitement," it will under-rate this audience. Weight behavioural intent over stated enthusiasm when sizing trade opportunity.
